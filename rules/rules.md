# *Rules v0.01*
So for the start, I have base all of my rules on the MITRE attack framework. Since I wanted to cover all the Tactics I had the "genius" idea to write all of them in one day and use A.I., even though I had some second thoughts about this I proceeded to do just that. Now I have 135 rules that are to broad and created a lot of false-positives. I have decided to disable all of them and focus on a few rules that actually are usable.
